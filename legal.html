About PAIRLY

PAIRLY is an adults-only (18+) marketplace where verified people can book other verified adults for friendly, platonic activities such as coffee, shopping, events and city tours. PAIRLY does not offer or allow sexual or romantic services of any kind.

Contact

Support email: awaiting configuration. To report a problem about a person, use Report or Block inside a booking chat. To open a payment or booking dispute, use Open dispute on the booking.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account details: email, display name, date of birth, city and interests.
  • Companion profile: bio, languages, packages, availability.
  • Booking details, in-app messages, reviews, reports and disputes.
  • Payment records: amount, method and transaction ID (we do not store card or wallet PINs).
  • An identity document photo, only if you submit one for verification.

How we use it

To run your account, confirm you are an adult, match and book companions, process payments, keep people safe and resolve disputes.

Who can see it

  • Other members see only a verified, published companion profile (name, city, bio, interests, packages, ratings).
  • Booking partners see each other's display name and the booking chat.
  • PAIRLY admins can view submitted ID photos for verification, reports, and chats of bookings under dispute. Admins do not read other chats.
  • Your phone number is never shown publicly. Messages containing phone numbers or links are blocked.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored with Supabase (Singapore region). ID photos are kept in a private store. We do not sell your data. We may share information with authorities when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can edit your name, city and interests in your profile. To delete your account or ID photo, contact support (email awaiting configuration).

Terms and Conditions

  • Adults only. You must be 18 or older and give a truthful date of birth. Accounts that are under age or fake are removed.
  • Platonic only. Sexual services, romantic or physical intimacy, escort-style offers, and requests for them are banned and lead to immediate removal.
  • Companions are independent. They set their own prices and conditions. PAIRLY is a marketplace, not their employer.
  • Bookings. A booking is confirmed when the companion accepts. The customer can cancel while a booking is requested or accepted. A detailed refund policy is awaiting configuration.
  • Payments. Pay only through the steps shown in the app and keep your transaction ID. Do not pay or arrange payment outside PAIRLY.
  • Conduct. No harassment, threats, discrimination, scams, sharing others' private details, or contacting people outside the app against their wishes.
  • Disputes. Either side can open a dispute. Admins review it and may confirm, reject or refund payments, suspend accounts, or remove profiles.
  • Suspension. We may suspend or remove accounts that break these terms.
  • Liability. Members meet at their own responsibility. Liability limits and governing law (Bangladesh) are to be confirmed by a lawyer.

Safety Guidelines

  • Meet only in public places and tell a friend or family member where you are going.
  • Keep chatting inside PAIRLY until you feel comfortable. Never share your phone number, address or passwords.
  • Check the companion's verified badge, reviews and level before booking.
  • Never send money outside the app's payment steps. Be careful with anyone who rushes you.
  • You can leave at any time if you feel uncomfortable.
  • Use Block and Report in the booking chat for anything suspicious or unsafe.
  • In an emergency, call 999 (Bangladesh) first, then tell us.
